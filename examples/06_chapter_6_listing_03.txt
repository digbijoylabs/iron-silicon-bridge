* Reference Blueprint: HLASM VSAM Control Block Access Kernel
MAIN     CSECT
         OPEN  (MYACB)
         MODCB RPL=MYRPL,                      <2>X
               OPTCD=(KEY,DIR,SYN,NUP),        X
               ARG=KEYADDR,                    X
               AREA=VSAMBUF
         GET   RPL=MYRPL
         LTR   R15,R15
         BNZ   VSAM_ERR
         CLOSE (MYACB)
         BR    R14

* VSAM Control Block Declarations
MYACB    ACB   AM=VSAM,MACRF=(KEY,DIR,IN)
MYRPL    RPL   ACB=MYACB,AM=VSAM,AREA=VSAMBUF
KEYADDR  DS    CL16
VSAMBUF  DS    CL1024
